Sudoku Loop Finder

Load a real puzzle, tap a technique, and see the loop of candidates that makes it work. A candidate is a small digit in an empty cell: a digit that could still go there. Each move comes with the chain of forced steps behind it. Press Play all steps to watch the whole puzzle being solved.
